# Home page surface brief

Scope: `/` (src/app/page.tsx). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: analytics/finance recruiters first, technical recruiters second; they scan in seconds and need to forward a résumé. Constraints: content from the content service only; no invented claims; HTTPS/VM/DNS untouched. User asked: modern, engaging, "wow this looks cool", real interactivity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A résumé read as a consulting deck: every section leads with the takeaway, every number is an exhibit. Refuses the chronological single-column résumé dump.

OWN-WORLD: Deep deck-navy ground in the hero, white slide panels below, one electric cobalt signal plus an amber second line for the business lens. Archivo (wide, heavy) for action titles, Hanken Grotesk for body, tabular figures in fixed-width slots. Hairline exhibit rules, source-note captions, no cards-in-cards.

STORY: Recruiters see "business + technical" in the first viewport, verify it with three real exhibits, switch the lens to their role, then download the résumé or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed navy. Name small top-left with résumé download (primary) and email copy (secondary) top-right. A monumental two-line action title naming analytics and full-stack. Below it a three-slot proof exhibit (20% fewer file errors · 1st place regional datathon · 1,732 dispatch records) whose numbers count into fixed slots. Lens switch: Both · Business · Technical.

FORM: Consulting exhibit deck, position 1 on the grounded list (user overrode the roll's assignment #5); seed key dadad15a. Raises: fixed-slot numerals (from seven-segment); monumental negative space around the action title (from fashion flagship). Signature interaction: the role lens re-lights skills, projects, and bullets by business vs technical; deck index tracks scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
